Slide 1: Title

Presenter 1 "Good morning, panelists. We are Group [Name], and today we present Kirby's Hardware POS and Inventory Management System. This is a browser-based, offline-capable, and AI-assisted platform designed specifically for small retail enterprises."

Slide 2: The Problem

Presenter 1 "Currently, hardware stores rely on handwritten ledgers. This leads to data loss, inventory drift, and a lack of business intelligence. Commercial POS systems are too expensive, and stable internet is not always guaranteed."

Slide 3 & 4: Functionality & Design

Presenter 2 "To solve this, we developed a system with core functionalities: a Point of Sale, Inventory Management, Reports, and strict Audit Trails. For the design, we prioritized a responsive UI that works on any device. As you can see, we have a clean two-column POS layout and a dashboard with real-time Chart.js visuals and a dark/light mode toggle."

Slide 5 & 6: Innovation & Tech Stack

Presenter 3 "What makes our system innovative is the integration of 'Kirby', an AI Assistant powered by Gemini and Groq, and a statistical forecasting engine. Technically, we used Vanilla JS for the client, a Serverless Python API on Vercel, and Google Cloud Firestore. This ensures zero hardware costs and a highly scalable architecture."

Slide 7: Database

Presenter 4 "Our database uses Firestore NoSQL. To prevent inventory drift, we use `runTransaction()` to guarantee that stock is decremented if and only if a sale is recorded. Security is enforced via Firebase Auth with strict Role-Based Access Control rules."

Slide 8: LIVE DEMONSTRATION (Realistic Scenario)

Setup: Presenter 4 is the Admin/Owner. Presenter 2 is the Cashier. Presenter 1 is a Contractor (Customer). Presenter 3 is Narrating.

Presenter 3 (Narrator) "For our live demo, we will simulate a real transaction. A contractor walks into the store to buy PVC pipes."

Presenter 1 (Customer) "Hi, I need to buy 5 pieces of the 2-inch PVC pipe."

Presenter 2 (Cashier) "Sure. I'll log in as the Cashier. I'll search for 'PVC' and since it has variants, I'll select the 2-inch option. I'll add a quantity of 5 to the cart. The system automatically calculates the 8% tax."

Presenter 3 (Narrator) "Notice that the Cashier does not have access to the Inventory or Audit pages, showing Role-Based Access."

Presenter 1 (Customer) "I'll pay using GCash."

Presenter 2 (Cashier) "I'll select GCash, input the 13-digit reference number, and confirm the payment. The system generates a receipt, and the Firestore transaction runs in the background."

Presenter 3 (Narrator) "Now, let's look at the Owner's view. Admin, what do you see?"

Presenter 4 (Admin) "I am logged in as the Admin. On my dashboard, the revenue updated instantly. If I go to Inventory, the stock for the 2-inch PVC dropped by 5. Because it hit the threshold, a low-stock alert appeared."

Presenter 4 (Admin) "I can also ask the AI assistant: 'What should I restock?' ... Kirby correctly identifies the PVC pipe based on the 30-day velocity."

Presenter 3 (Narrator) "Finally, let's test the offline capability. Cashier, disconnect the Wi-Fi."

Presenter 2 (Cashier) "Wi-Fi is off. I'll process another sale... and the receipt still generates. The system queued the sale locally."

Presenter 3 (Narrator) "When the connection returns, the background flush syncs the data automatically without duplicating the transaction. That concludes our demonstration."

Slide 9: Conclusion & Q&A

Presenter 1 "To conclude, this system transforms manual retail into a fully digital, AI-assisted workflow. It guarantees business continuity offline and eliminates the need for expensive hardware. Thank you, we are ready for your questions."

Database & Data Management

inventory

Products, Variants, Stock Levels

orders

Receipts, Payments, Line Items

customers

Profiles, Spend, Order Count

audit_logs

User Actions, Timestamps

  • Data Integrity: POS sales use runTransaction() to guarantee that stock is decremented if and only if an order is successfully recorded.
  • Security Rules: All collections require request.auth != null. Admin-only mutations are strictly enforced at the database layer.
  • Offline Sync: Firestore persistence queues supported writes in IndexedDB and replays them automatically on reconnect.
